have different reuse needs. This mapping is the authoritative file-scope policy.

[Apache License 2.0](../LICENSE):

## Project-authored documents and data — CC BY 4.0

The following paths are licensed under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](CC-BY-4.0.txt):

- `README.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and other project-authored root documentation

- `research/`
- `schemas/`
- `skills/`
- `standard/`
- this licensing policy

The following is a non-exhaustive operational summary (비완전 요약). The full
license text controls. When sharing or adapting this material, retain any
supplied creator identification, copyright notice (저작권 고지), license notice
or license URI, disclaimer notice (면책 고지), and source URI. Identify the
project as `preo contributors`, link to <https://github.com/domuk-k/preo>,
identify CC BY 4.0, and indicate changes. You can provide these details in any
reasonable manner permitted by the license.

## Boundaries and third-party material

- License texts retain their own terms and are not relicensed by this policy.
- A URL, citation, summary, or metadata record does not grant rights to the
  external source it describes.
- Each third-party source keeps its original terms. Repository licenses apply
  only to material the preo contributors have the right to license.
- No ASD logo, ASD-STE100 PDF, controlled dictionary, paid paper, or paid book
  is redistributed.
- Files stored locally under `research/sources/` are excluded from Git and from
  this repository's licensed distribution.

Contribution licensing and source-provenance requirements are in
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](../CONTRIBUTING.md).
